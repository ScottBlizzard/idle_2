# Multi-agent anomaly-first reset

Date: 31 August 2026  
Decision: **`NO_ACTIVE_SEED — STOP_ATLAS_MAPPING — BUILD_RESIDUAL_LEDGER`**

## Executive conclusion

Three independent searches agree on the same diagnosis. The project is not failing
because the snooker discussion was shallow or because one more analogy is missing. It is
failing because it repeatedly starts with a desirable abstract mechanism and then looks
for an AI carrier. That process generates coherent ideas inside the most mature parts of
current literature. Combining more source residues improves causal vocabulary but does
not create a new invariant.

No current candidate is an ICLR Oral seed. The correct reset is to find a stable empirical
residual first, reproduce it cheaply, and only then use the Atlas to interpret or reject
mechanisms.

## What the three independent tracks found

### 1. Multi-residue search

The best joint constructions were:

- clarification as a capability-improving channel that expands prompt-injection
  consequences only when clarification participates in control flow;
- deletion of a currently dormant structure that is harmless under the current
  obligation but collapses finite-step adaptation after an obligation change.

Both are closed. ASPI and 2026 agent-security frameworks already isolate the first
channel. Hidden Gauge, function-preserving growth, and gradient-based plasticity already
occupy the second. More generally:

- if an endogenous fallback leaves the realized candidate set and information unchanged,
  its earlier participation is causally screened off;
- if two present-equivalent models also have the same future update operator, their
  finite-step reachable function sets are identical by induction. A nonzero difference
  is therefore an unmatched learning-geometry/plasticity difference.

This explains why stacking residues did not raise novelty.

### 2. Hostile reset outside snooker

The reset produced zero paper seeds. It identified six recurrent failure modes:

1. concept-first rather than anomaly-first search;
2. applying final Oral standards before any phenomenon is observed;
3. choosing variables that cannot be changed without changing information or semantics;
4. treating a predictor as if it were an actuator;
5. ending with an obvious selector/router/regularizer/schedule;
6. searching paper headlines after the headline anomaly has already been published.

The receiver-NLL experiment is the cleanest internal example: natural NLL strongly
predicted error retention, yet the matched whitespace intervention moved NLL without
moving retention. Prediction did not identify a causal coordinate.

### 3. Phenomenon-first carrier search

Only two anomaly scanners survived literature triage, not as paper seeds:

- re-entrant sign changes in strict metamorphic effects across public pretraining
  checkpoints and random seeds;
- sign reversal in the value of the same correct hint under logically dual obligations.

The checkpoint route ranked first because PolyPythias supplies 9 seeds and 154 aligned
checkpoints per model. It was therefore implemented and preflighted rather than promoted
from prose.

## What the implementation established

### V1: three strict-equivalence families

The implementation froze 288 pairs in Boolean De Morgan, directed-graph bijection, and
linear-reparameterization families. The final-checkpoint seed-1 preflight completed all
576 scoring records, but no family cleared the frozen 60% two-representation capability
gate:

| family | original | transformed |
|---|---:|---:|
| Boolean De Morgan | 55.21% | 58.33% |
| graph bijection | 40.62% | 42.71% |
| linear reparameterization | 53.12% | 39.58% |

Discovery was not started and no longitudinal effect was inspected.

An adversarial code review separately found that v1's majority gate could pass without
the same seeds reproducing a complete three-lobe trajectory; confirmation lacked an
executable seal; exact completeness and source/model hashes were not enforced; and one
checkpoint spike could count as a lobe. These defects are documented even though v1
stopped before they affected a result.

### Carrier calibration after v1

The gate was not lowered. Two simpler candidate carriers were tested only for final-model
capability:

1. **Key-value lookup, target first versus last.** Three of four template/representation
   cells reached 72.66%–91.41%, but one reached only 66.41%; the large order imbalance
   itself makes the carrier unsuitable for a balanced phase scan.
2. **Small-integer addition associativity.** Moving only parentheses kept operands,
   order, operators, truth, and labels fixed, but both representations scored exactly
   50%. Pythia-410M did not perform the base computation.

Further simplification would approach copying or lexical matching and would remove the
scientific content. The PolyPythias re-entry route is therefore closed, not enlarged.

## New discovery protocol

### A. Residual ledger

Mine recent papers with public artifacts for exact unexplained cells, not abstract claims:

- sign reversals and non-monotonicity;
- one capable family moving opposite to another;
- a key ablation contradicting the proposed mechanism;
- better data, more compute, or a stronger component making the result worse;
- the same artifact changing sign across channel or stage;
- discrete budget, scale, or training-phase crossovers;
- results explicitly described as surprising, unclear, or inconsistent.

Each ledger row must include the exact table/figure, effect, seeds, public artifact,
author explanation, one refuting counterfactual, collision status, and minimum rerun cost.

### B. Blind reproduction funnel

Select at most four anomalies, each capped at one aggregate GPU-hour. Freeze the exact
direction, minimum magnitude, two seeds, and one adjacent condition. Do not tune a prompt,
model, parser, checkpoint, or subgroup after observing the replication.

### C. Causal cube

Only replicated anomalies receive a mechanism study. State three competing explanations
and build the smallest intervention in which they predict different signs. Equivalence
matching belongs here, after a real phenomenon exists, rather than as a precondition for
inventing one.

### D. Oral gate

Promote an anomaly only if the mechanism predicts a new held-out reversal boundary,
ordinary explanations fail, exact-neighbor literature does not already contain the
intervention, and the consequence is stronger than a local method knob.

## Binding next action

Do not run more Atlas-to-carrier mappings, a logical-dual hint experiment, a simplified
PolyPythias task, or another broad Pro brainstorm. Build the residual ledger from primary
papers and supplements, then authorize at most four one-hour reproductions. The Atlas is
retained as a mechanism vocabulary after anomalies are found, not as a seed generator.
