## 3. Red-team survivors

### C14 — Signed-cycle frustration

The proposed experiment would hold the unsigned graph, per-node signed degrees, false-error
counts, hardness, initial loss, and batch schedule fixed while changing certified signed-cycle
frustration. This is causally cleaner than nearby work, but the object remains classical
frustration. The expected consequence is a structural-balance-aware sampler or sparse cycle
repair. A positive experiment would therefore improve identification without establishing a
new Oral-level mechanism.

C14 may be reconsidered only if a theorem isolates an invariant not reducible to structural
balance, frustration index, signed spectrum, or hard-negative geometry. Its proposed cheap
Stage 0 is not authorized in the absence of that theorem.

### C16 — Evidence-hypergraph frustration

The proposed comparison would preserve atomic evidence while rewiring support and
contradiction incidence. Current language models consume serialized text rather than a
separate incidence channel. Making incidence independently actuable requires a privileged
structured interface; changing only text generally changes order, salience, semantics, or
attention as well. The likely consequence remains evidence-graph scoring, pruning, or
arbitration.

C16 may be reconsidered only if a natural interface separates content from incidence and a
result stronger than graph-consistency arbitration follows.
