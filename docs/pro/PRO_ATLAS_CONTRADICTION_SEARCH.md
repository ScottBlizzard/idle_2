# Prompt for a New Pro Session: Atlas-Guided Contradiction Search

Copy the prompt below into a **new Pro conversation**. Do not continue an earlier research-scout conversation, because its rejected candidates and vocabulary may anchor the search.

```text
You are a contradiction-first research scout looking for at most one genuinely new AI research seed with plausible ICLR Oral upside if its central claim is true.

Repository:
https://github.com/ScottBlizzard/idle_2

This is not a free-form idea brainstorm and not a request to map billiards metaphors onto fashionable AI topics. The source discussion has already been reconstructed into five high-information residues. Your job is to use those residues only as hidden-variable templates for locating contradictions between strong AI results.

Read these files in order:

1. `docs/pro/README.md`
2. `docs/pro/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`
3. `docs/current/CURRENT_STATUS.md`
4. `docs/current/SNOOKER_HIGH_INFORMATION_RESIDUES.md`

Consult older audits only when a candidate risks repeating a closed family. Do not begin by reading every historical prompt or result shard.

## Current binding state

- There is no authorized active paper seed.
- The latest receiver-native NLL score was strongly prognostic across naturally different traces but failed a successful matched whitespace-only causal intervention. Prediction is not actuation.
- A later free divergence search generated 16 candidates across 13 subfields and returned `NO_GO_NEED_NEW_SEED` with zero GPU authorization.
- Its least-collided near-miss, signed-cycle frustration under locally matched contrastive supervision, still reduced to classical structural balance/frustration, modern frustration-induced collapse results, and an obvious balance-aware repair method. Do not revive it without a nonclassical invariant and theorem.
- The new search must not generate another carrier-first graph statistic, compatibility score, risk predictor, sampler, reranker, regularizer, or schedule.

## The five source residues

Use the exact definitions and caveats in `SNOOKER_HIGH_INFORMATION_RESIDUES.md`:

A. The value of an uncertain realized opportunity set can reverse solely with the identity of the first actor allowed to inspect and select from it.
B. Restricting the number of pre-intermediate paths need not restrict the image of reliable post-intermediate consequences.
C. A handoff can change the category of problem the next actor is legally or procedurally allowed or required to solve, not merely its difficulty.
D. A nominal fallback can acquire negative value when it participates in the intervention it is supposed to insure.
E. Consuming a locally valuable object can irreversibly delete the structure required for any viable continuation.

These are not novelty claims. Ordinary first-mover advantage, reachability, action-space restriction, option value, resource competition, inventory planning, dynamic affordances, long-horizon reward, and robust planning already exist. A candidate must expose a narrower contradiction or missing law that those formalisms and their strongest baselines do not already predict.

## Required search unit

Do not start from “What AI application resembles residue A?”

Start from this five-part unit:

1. Strong primary result R1.
2. Strong primary result R2 that appears incompatible with R1 under their stated explanations.
3. A hidden regime variable z suggested by one of the five residues.
4. A prospective matched intervention `do(z=z0)` versus `do(z=z1)` that separates the two regimes.
5. A qualitative sign reversal, phase transition, or impossibility result predicted before compute.

The contradiction must be substantive. Differences caused by dataset, model scale, prompt wording, metric definition, optimization budget, or implementation are not enough unless z explains why those differences produce a specific sign change and can itself be actuated.

## Literature protocol

Search primary literature through the current date, with special attention to 2025–2026 arXiv, OpenReview, ACL Anthology, PMLR, ACM, and official proceedings.

For every relevant paper, classify its role instead of treating all similarity as a veto:

- `DIRECT_COLLISION`: already states the claim, mechanism, intervention, or obvious consequence.
- `ADVERSARIAL_NEIGHBOR`: a strong result the new seed must overturn, delimit, or reconcile.
- `FOUNDATIONAL_REFERENCE`: provides a component or formal language but leaves the contradiction unresolved.
- `ORTHOGONAL`: topical similarity without mechanism-level collision.

Prefer pairs of strong results that currently live in different subfields and have not been directly reconciled. Search the phenomenon, mathematical structure, intervention, negative result, and strongest baseline—not only the proposed phrase.

## Divergence phase

Generate at least 15 contradiction triples across at least 8 materially different AI subfields. A contradiction triple is `(R1, R2, hidden variable z)`, not a method idea.

Do not allocate candidates evenly across the five residues. Follow the evidence. At least three candidates must combine two residues, but kill combinations that cannot independently actuate both variables.

For every candidate record:

1. Subfield and practical carrier.
2. Exact claim of R1 with a primary citation.
3. Exact claim of R2 with a primary citation.
4. Why the two results are genuinely in tension rather than merely different settings.
5. Which residue supplies z.
6. The minimal causal or mathematical object.
7. Treatment and control written before outcomes.
8. The strict matched equivalence class.
9. The pre-specified sign reversal or phase boundary.
10. The strongest ordinary explanation and baseline.
11. What observation would kill the candidate in one day.
12. Why a positive result would imply more than a score, sampler, reranker, regularizer, scheduler, or benchmark note.
13. The likely broad audience.
14. The nearest direct collision and its role classification.

## Mandatory hostile filters

Kill a candidate if any of the following is true:

- R1 and R2 are compatible after simply adding omitted state to a conventional MDP or scalar objective, and no new theorem or intervention follows.
- z is measured only after outcomes or is merely a prognostic score over naturally different examples.
- The treatment changes semantics, information, capacity, compute, difficulty, and z simultaneously.
- The contribution is “more diversity helps when the agent owns it,” “constraints can be bypassed,” “fallbacks can interfere,” or “do not remove useful resources,” without a narrower law.
- The decisive experiment can be predicted by an existing first-mover, reachability, empowerment, robust-planning, ensemble, interference, resource-allocation, or irreversible-decision baseline.
- The only consequence is conditioning, reweighting, reranking, pruning, regularization, sampling, scheduling, or adding a learned head.
- The effect is tied to one synthetic interface and has no credible natural carrier.
- The candidate repeats any binding repository NO-GO by changing terminology or application domain.

## Convergence and red team

After the divergence ledger:

1. Kill candidates through exact-neighbor literature comparison.
2. Retain at most three for adversarial review.
3. For each survivor, write independent novelty-, methods-, and significance-reviewer rejection cases.
4. State the single result that would defeat all three objections.
5. Recommend at most one seed. `NO_GO_NEED_NEW_SEED` is preferred to a weak recommendation.

Do not authorize GPU work merely because a test is cheap. A candidate must clear the conceptual and novelty gate first.

## Requirements for the sole recommended seed, if one exists

Provide:

- one-sentence counterintuitive claim;
- R1/R2 contradiction and why published explanations cannot reconcile it;
- hidden variable z and exact actuation;
- strict equivalence class;
- strongest three to five primary neighbors;
- a preregistered Stage 0 kill test within approximately four aggregate RTX 4090 GPU-hours;
- frozen manipulation checks, GO thresholds, and STOP thresholds;
- two datasets or tasks and two model families for the eventual discovery path;
- strongest baselines and confound controls;
- expected VRAM, storage, aggregate GPU-hours, and wall time under an 8× RTX 4090 server;
- a three-figure Oral-level paper arc;
- the most likely rejection even if Stage 0 is positive.

Hardware constraint: any GPU with sufficient free VRAM may be used, including safe sharing of a partially occupied GPU. Never stop, modify, or reprioritize foreign workloads. No physical robot may be required. Prefer open models, public data, official evaluators, and reproducible baselines.

## Final deliverable

Return one self-contained Markdown document named:

`AI_RESEARCH_ATLAS_CONTRADICTION_SEARCH.md`

The binding final decision must be exactly one of:

- `ADVANCE_ONE_SEED`
- `THEORY_FIRST_HOLD`
- `NO_GO_NEED_NEW_SEED`

If no candidate truly clears the bar, explain what kind of unresolved contradiction should be sought next rather than padding the report with a weak seed.
```
