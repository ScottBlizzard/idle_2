# Crossed Repair Pilot Protocol

## 0. Decision boundary

Status: `PREREGISTRATION DRAFT — NO RUN WHILE SSH IS UNAVAILABLE`.

The pilot is designed to kill, not confirm, two nested claims:

1. model-specific own-failure correction rates confound repair competence with an
   endogenous failure cohort;
2. after standardization, error difficulty contains a reproducible
   generator-family-by-corrector-family interaction that can support complementary
   routing.

Claim 1 alone is not an Oral-level seed. Claim 2 plus an equal-budget routing consequence
is required for promotion.

## 1. Frozen public source

Use the ICLR 2026 `YefanZhou98/LLMVerify-Generator` dataset. It contains approximately
619,000 labeled trajectories from 15 generators over 12 benchmarks, including GSM8K and
MATH-500. The source already crosses generator families and sizes and labels final answers
by benchmark-specific extraction rather than an LLM judge.

Record the dataset revision hash before acquisition. Never overwrite a downloaded source
snapshot.

## 2. Models

Primary 2-family-by-2-size grid:

| family | smaller | larger |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen | Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct | Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct |
| Gemma | gemma-2-2b-it | gemma-2-9b-it |

The exact same four model IDs must appear as source generators and as correctors. Before
calling a diagonal cell "self correction," verify that the public dataset records the
same model revision as the locally frozen corrector. If this cannot be established, call
it a `matched_model_id` diagonal rather than genuine ownership. All four models fit
individually on a 24 GB RTX 4090. Use only physical GPUs 4--7 and never stop or modify a
foreign process.

If one model cannot be acquired or loaded without changing precision across cells, stop
the pilot. Do not substitute a model after outcomes are visible.

## 3. Error-bank construction

Domains: GSM8K and MATH-500.

For each domain and generator:

1. identify problems on which all four generators have at least one labeled incorrect
   trajectory in the public bundle;
2. choose at most one incorrect trajectory per `(generator, problem)` using a frozen hash
   order;
3. stratify by the public per-problem generator-difficulty score and response length;
4. retain 60 errors per generator per domain, for 480 unique error traces total;
5. preserve question, erroneous response, extracted wrong answer, gold answer, source ID,
   generator identity, and SHA-256 of the raw error text.

Gold answers are used only by the evaluator, never placed in the correction prompt.

If fewer than 40 errors per generator per domain survive the common-item requirement, stop
and report an underpowered pilot. Do not relax to generator-specific problem sets.

## 4. Crossed interventions

Every retained error is sent to all four correctors under both wrappers:

- `external_neutral`: the trace is described as a proposed solution from an unspecified
  source;
- `assistant_history`: the same raw trace is placed in the assistant-history position and
  the model is asked to check the preceding solution. The prompt never claims that an
  off-diagonal trace was actually generated by the current corrector.

The raw question and error bytes must be identical across wrappers before chat-template
application. Store their hashes. The wrapper contrast is a presentation-role control,
not evidence of psychological ownership. Use greedy decoding, one continuation, and the
same output budget in every cell. The model must return a final answer in the benchmark's
expected format. No error-location hint and no gold feedback are provided.

Total primary calls: `480 errors × 4 correctors × 2 wrappers = 3,840`.

## 5. Outcomes and models

Primary binary outcome: corrected final answer matches the benchmark verifier.

Report:

1. diagonal own-model repair rate;
2. each corrector's rate on the pooled common error bank;
3. generator-column and corrector-row effects;
4. wrapper effect;
5. generator-family-by-corrector-family interaction.

Fit a preregistered logistic model with problem and error-instance fixed effects:

\[
\operatorname{logit}P(Y=1)=u_{problem}+v_{error}+\alpha_{corrector}
+\rho_{wrapper}+\eta_{generator\ family,corrector\ family}.
\]

Use problem-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals. Report raw cell counts and paired
McNemar contrasts as well as the model estimate.

## 6. Automatic gate

`KILL_NO_SELECTION_REVERSAL` if standardized corrector-size ordering does not differ from
the diagonal ordering by at least 5 percentage points in either domain.

`AUDIT_ONLY_NO_RELATIONAL_DEPTH` if selection reversal occurs but all family-interaction
contrasts are smaller than 5 points or their 95% problem-clustered intervals cross zero in
both domains.

`KILL_ROLE_ONLY` if the apparent interaction disappears under `external_neutral` and is
fully explained by the wrapper.

`ADVANCE_ROUTER_TEST` only if:

- standardized correction scales positively with corrector size;
- a same-family or pair-specific interaction of at least 5 points replicates in direction
  across both domains;
- the interaction remains after problem, error, length, and public difficulty controls;
- no cell has a parser or truncation failure rate above 2%.

## 7. Router consequence

Only after `ADVANCE_ROUTER_TEST`, train a router on one split to choose one corrector per
error under a one-call budget. Test on held-out problems and compare against:

- always use the largest corrector;
- random corrector;
- random cross-family corrector;
- size-only routing;
- error-type routing;
- majority voting at an explicitly higher call budget.

Promotion beyond a pilot requires at least a 3-point held-out gain over the largest
one-call corrector in both domains without using generator identity unavailable at
deployment. The deployable router may use only trace text, question, and source-agnostic
features.

## 8. Resource and integrity constraints

- Physical GPUs 0--3 are out of scope.
- Use only physical GPUs 4--7 after 180 seconds of exclusive idleness.
- Never stop foreign jobs.
- Freeze code, prompts, dataset revision, model revisions, and selection hashes before
  reading correction outcomes.
- Failed engineering attempts receive new versioned output directories.
- Do not weaken gates after observing data.
